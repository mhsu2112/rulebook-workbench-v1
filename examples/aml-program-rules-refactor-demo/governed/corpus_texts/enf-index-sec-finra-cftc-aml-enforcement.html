Administrative Proceedings

The links below direct you to the individual case pages for litigated administrative proceedings adjudicated by the Commission, including its consideration of appeals from initial decisions issued by administrative law judges or from SRO or PCAOB action. The case pages contain the Commission opinions and orders as well as significant filings in such matters. The links below additionally direct you to the electronic filing system for administrative proceedings and to information for respondents in administrative proceedings.

  • Litigated AP Case Materials (Open)
  • Litigated AP Case Materials (Closed)
  • Electronic Filings in Administrative Proceedings (eFAP)
  • Information for Respondents in Administrative Proceedings
  • Subpoena Form to Produce Documents - Proceeding Before the Commission
  • Subpoena Form to Appear and Testify at a Deposition - Proceeding Before the Commission
 

Below is a chronological list of links to orders by the Commission instituting both litigated and settled administrative proceedings and to issuances for the Commission, by the Division of Enforcement, pursuant to delegated authority.  The orders and other issuances in litigated administrative proceedings below also may be found on the individual case pages linked on the Open Litigated Administrative Proceedings and Closed Litigated Administrative Proceedings pages, together with filings made in the proceedings.  The issuances pertaining to distribution proceedings may also be found in the individual case charts on the Distributions in Commission Administrative Proceedings: Notices and Orders Pertaining to Disgorgement and Fair Funds page.
