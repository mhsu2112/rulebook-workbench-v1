Mode: refactor · 170 finalized operations · effect classes: clarify 60, codify 97, fill_gap 13 · rendered 2026-07-21 02:48 UTC
Ratified scope. Distill the current federal AML/CFT program-establishment obligations (the program/'pillars' rules) for U.S. depository institutions, money services businesses, and broker-dealers (including the FINRA SRO layer) under FinCEN, the federal banking agencies, and the SEC as of a pinned 2026 date into a derived blueprint, defect register, and refactored target blueprint with operation trace for the Foundry working group, using statutes, regulations, SRO rules, official guidance, and examination manuals as authority and enforcement actions as evidence only, treating other covered sectors (casinos, mutual funds, insurance, etc.), OFAC/sanctions, fraud, and state AML/licensing as boundary regimes and SAR/CTR/CDD operational duties as coordination layer.
This is the Target Blueprint in operation-trace form: the Derived Blueprint (its element tables) plus the ordered, finalized operations below, each with the human disposition that approved it. A fully materialized composite — the element tables rewritten — is a later step (backlog B6). Provision-level accountability (where every legacy rule went) is in the Crosswalk (Align tab); the underlying element tables are in the Derived Blueprint.
